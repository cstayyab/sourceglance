    Lists

    Organizations

    Name each organization. Organizations with the same name share a group in your lists. This stays in Source Glance; nothing changes on GitHub or GitLab.

      Sign in to see your organizations.

      Projects

      Reset projects? This removes every project you made or renamed and puts moved repositories back. Each repository becomes a project of its own again, submodules rejoin their parent’s project, and projects from a platform stay. Organization names aren’t affected. You can’t undo this.

      Repositories are projects of their own until you group them: rename a project, or drag a repository onto another.

        Some accounts can’t read repository files, so submodules aren’t found. Drag them into a project, or sign in with a fine-grained token.

        Repositories to watch

        On github.com, your lists show the repositories you watch: all of them until you switch some off. Add a public repository to watch it too.
