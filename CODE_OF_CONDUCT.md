# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want sourceglance to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel
where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer at **hire.umer@gmail.com**. All reports are
reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may respond with a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on
the severity and history of the behaviour. Maintainers who don't follow this code in good
faith may be removed from the project.
